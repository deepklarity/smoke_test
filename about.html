Our Brand Story

Founded in 2022, Bean & Bloom was born from a simple idea: that a great cup of coffee should be enjoyed in a beautiful, inspiring environment. Our founders, lifelong coffee enthusiasts and amateur florists, wanted to create a space that celebrates both the art of the brew and the beauty of nature.

Every bean we serve is ethically sourced from small-scale farmers who prioritize quality and sustainability. Our floral arrangements are curated weekly from local growers, ensuring that every visit to Bean & Bloom is a fresh experience for all your senses.
