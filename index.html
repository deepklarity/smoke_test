Discover Paris

The City of Light awaits. Explore iconic landmarks, world-class museums, hidden neighbourhoods, and the timeless charm that has captivated travellers for centuries.

Why Paris?

From the iron lattice of the Eiffel Tower to the hallowed halls of the Louvre, Paris is a living museum of art, history, and joie de vivre. Here are six unmissable places to start your journey.

Landmark

Eiffel Tower

The iron lady of Paris — a wrought-iron masterpiece built for the 1889 World's Fair, offering breathtaking panoramic views of the city.

Art Museum

Louvre Museum

The world's largest art museum, home to the Mona Lisa and thousands of masterpieces spanning ancient civilisations to the 19th century.

Explore →
Cathedral

Notre Dame Cathedral

A Gothic masterpiece on the Île de la Cité — soaring flying buttresses, rose windows, and centuries of French history.

Explore →
Basilica

Sacré-Cœur Basilica

Perched atop Montmartre, this luminous white basilica offers sweeping views of Paris and one of the world's largest mosaics.

Explore →
Art Museum

Musée d'Orsay

Housed in a stunning Belle Époque railway station, this museum holds the world's finest collection of Impressionist art.

Explore →
Monument

Arc de Triomphe

A towering triumphal arch at the centre of Place Charles de Gaulle, commemorating French military glory with an eternal flame below.
